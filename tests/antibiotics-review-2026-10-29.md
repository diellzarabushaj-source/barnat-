# Antibiotikët — source and software review, 29 October 2026

Scope: 51 outpatient options across 13 indications (including one topical option and one procedure), 16 linked hospital regimens, 20 preparation references and the existing AKKPM form/strength integration. This review compares the published source rules and software behavior. It is not independent clinical certification, a Kosovo antibiogram or a complete renal/neonatal dosing service.

## Corrections

- Unspecified cephalosporin allergy no longer automatically exposes penicillins. The clinician must explicitly identify an unverified, non-anaphylactic, non-SCAR history without penicillin allergy. The same gate applies to the linked hospital choices and resets on reload/allergy change. See [AAAAI/ACAAI practice parameter, CBS 14–16](https://www.aaaai.org/Aaaai/media/Media-Library-PDFs/Allergist%20Resources/Statements%20and%20Practice%20Parameters/Drug-Allergy-PP-9-2022.pdf).
- The outpatient calculator deliberately excludes infants below three months; this conservative calculator scope does not claim that antibiotics are contraindicated at that age. Source-specific higher thresholds remain. The hospital module excludes neonates and withholds numeric dosing/preparation until age is confirmed, including records that previously omitted an age field. [UCSF separates dosing above one month from its neonatal schedules](https://idmp.ucsf.edu/pediatric-antimicrobial-dosing-benioff-childrens-hospitals).
- Sinusitis now retains the source's conditional severe-symptom duration and the finalizer can represent each permitted course length; it does not silently apply the longer course by default. See [Children's Mercy handbook, rhinosinusitis section](https://www.childrensmercy.org/siteassets/media-documents-for-depts-section/documents-for-health-care-providers/evidence-based-practice/clinical-practice-guidelines--care-process-models/outpatient-antibiotic-handbook.pdf).
- Bicillin L-A preparations require one complete matched prefilled syringe. Nominal syringe size is no longer shown as a partial-volume withdrawal instruction: the [product label](https://www.dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=012d46f1-d0a0-4676-a879-cd320297ab16) distinguishes nominal sizes from actual filled volumes. No IU-to-mg conversion is introduced.

## Interface

A compatible form and strength produces one answer containing amount per administration, frequency/equal interval, 24-hour total, course length and patient-specific ceiling. Duplicate mg/meta blocks disappear only while that complete answer is available; they return if the product, age or conversion becomes invalid. Calculations and source ceilings remain in native disclosures. Clinical conditions, reserve-agent indications, absent source maxima and allergy stops remain visible. Packaging moves under optional product details. mL rounding is identified; daily arithmetic uses unrounded mg. Phone and desktop use the same data and controls.
