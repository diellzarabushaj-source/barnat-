# Per-indication calculator and source coverage review — 2026-11-02

This change preserves the released catalog design, navigation and authentication and completes the independent workspace structure for all 387 named antibiotic/indication pairs. Entries without a compatible specific indication remain explicit review gaps. It does not claim 387 clinically approved dosing protocols.

Each workspace has its own product, source regimen, age/unit, weight and renal fields where relevant. Fields do not copy across indications or accounts and do not persist or transmit patient data. Age units include years/months/days; changing units clears the age rather than converting it silently. Local forms omit the weight field. Source/product details stay collapsed by default. Canonical indication labels remain unchanged; the ten indications with choice references are grouped first in the dropdown.

The user explicitly selected product-specific SmPC plus Mayo as dosing source policy. The AEMPS/CIMA SmPC for Ceftazidima Qilu 1 g (83901, October 2025, retrieved 2026-11-02) supplies a bounded reference for adult complicated UTI, intermittent IV, age 18–80 and weight ≥40 kg. The source dose range and interval alternatives stay separate; no regimen is chosen automatically and no course duration is invented. Renal impairment, dialysis, age >80, pediatric use and continuous infusion are outside this initial profile. The source is linked only to the exact Qilu product, strength, route/form and MA registration. It is not a CEFIN SmPC or Kosovo clinical approval. The current Mayo ceftazidime monograph supplies no numerical dose. Both original Mayo rows remain unchanged, including the explicitly blocked 2012 archive.

The arithmetic engine distinguishes fixed mg/dose, mg/kg/day and mg/kg/dose, applies only provided caps, rejects missing approval, stale sources, identity/route mismatch, unsupported intervals, invalid ages/weights, renal/dialysis exclusions and source conflicts. It does not derive volume, vial rounding, reconstitution, renal adjustments or treatment duration. Tests use synthetic, clearly labeled fixtures. The production approval registry is empty, so the UI remains unable to emit numerical dose results. Clinical activation requires a concrete product/protocol/source version and clinician approval; it is not inferred from this implementation request, favorites, source choice or Active registration.

Choice coverage increases from seven profiles/five IDs to twelve profiles/ten IDs, adding bounded lower UTI, pyelonephritis, cellulitis and impetigo references (NICE NG109/111/141/153). Missing trimethoprim/pivmecillinam monotherapies are reference-only notices outside the catalog. Co-trimoxazole is not substituted. IV-only flucloxacillin is not assigned to oral choices. Topical 2% references do not become nasal/eye or steroid-combination recommendations. Nitrofurantoin reference matching distinguishes MR 100 mg from ordinary 50 mg capsules; other strengths/forms are not treated as interchangeable.

The original 660 Active products and all 290 original Mayo rows retain their values. The source-form guard now recognizes granules; five tablet-only Midecamycin references no longer display on its granular presentation. All original reference records remain in the versioned Sheet snapshot. No SmPC dose is labeled as Mayo and no generic infection label gains a guessed dose.

Validation is recorded in the pull request after the registered complete suite and Chromium/WebKit integration runs. Targeted tests cover source/product specificity, independent indication drafts, age units, missing local forms, renal review, empty release approvals, dose-unit arithmetic and failure cases. The native Sheet receives the expanded choice review and a separate calculator coverage inventory, with no patient information and JO-only activation status.
